## Topic 3: Humidity Definitions and Normal Pathway

- **Humidity:** water in gaseous state.
- **Absolute humidity:** mg/L water vapor.
- **Relative humidity:** % of maximum possible at that temperature.
- **Potential humidity:** maximum water vapor at a given temperature.

**Normal pathway:**

- Nose: ~50% of BTPS.
- **BTPS:** 37°C, ambient pressure, saturated (100% RH).
- Upper airway/trachea: **32–34°C**, **100% RH**.
- Below **ISB:** gas reaches **37°C and 100% RH** (BTPS).

**ISB:**

- Location: **5 cm below carina** (~2nd/3rd bronchial divisions).
- Position fluctuates with exertion, breathing rate, inspired air temperature.
- EIA affects ISB location.

**Purpose:**

- Reduce humidity/temperature deficits → prevent inspissated secretions and obstruction.

---

## Topic 4: Medical Gas and Artificial Airways

- Medical gas: **0% humidity** — must add humidity to all medical gases.
- Provide **> 60%** humidity to upper airways and below to avoid lung injury.

**ISB principles:**

- Inspiration: temperature and RH increase.
- Exhalation: temperature and RH decrease.
- Below ISB: constant **37°C BTPS**.

**Trach/ET tubes:**

- Upper airway bypassed → add heat and humidity.
- Cold dry gas ↓ ciliary motility, ↑ mucus, inspissated secretions.

## Topic 6: HME (Passive)

**Function:** recapture exhaled heat/humidity; returns on inspiration. Hydration status affects output.

| Type | Efficiency |
|------|------------|
| Simple condenser | 50% |
| Hygroscopic condenser | 70% |
| Hydrophobic condenser | ~70%, anti-bacterial, higher WOB |

**Indications:**

- Short-term transport on ventilator.
- SNF up to 1 week.
- Trach patients off ventilator during transport.
- Reduced circuit colonization.

**Contraindications:**

- Thick/copious/bloody secretions.
- Temp < **32°C (89.6°F)**.
- Minute ventilation **> 10 L/min**.
- Pediatric/neonatal (dead space).
- **COPD** (↑ resistance, WOB, trapping, acidosis).

## Topic 7: Active Humidifiers

### Bubble

- Low-flow O2 devices; gas below water surface; diffuser increases surface area.

### Pass-over

- Heated chamber; contact with warm water surface.
- **37°C → 44 mg H2O/L**; **31°C → 30 mg H2O/L**.
- Invasive and non-invasive ventilation.
- Heated optimal; cool ~20 mg/L at 22°C; disposable vs reusable.

### Wick

- Most humidity for invasive/non-invasive ventilation (Concha Therm, wick canister).

## Topic 8: Bland Aerosol Therapy (LVN)

**Aerosol:** particles suspended in gas.

**LVN:**

- Aerosolized water to upper airways.
- O2 up to **100%** at flows **> 100 L/min**.
- Heated aerosol for artificial airway (trach).

**Cool aerosol goals:**

- Upper airway inflammation (intubation, trauma, croup, epiglottitis).
- Vasoconstriction, ↓ inflammation, ↑ large airway airflow, ↓ WOB.

**Warm aerosol goals:**

- Prevent dry gas injury to large airways.
- Preserve mucociliary escalator.
- ↑ gas temperature → ↑ water carrying capacity.
- Mucosal hydration when upper airway bypassed (trach off vent).

**Indications summary:**

- Upper airway edema, laryngotracheobronchitis, subglottic edema, post-extubation edema, post-op upper airway, bypassed airway (heated), sputum specimen/mobilization.

## Topic 9: High-Yield Review

| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| Passover, wick, bland aerosol, bubble, LVN | HME |

- ISB ~5 cm below carina; BTPS 37°C, 100% RH.
- Medical gas 0% humidity; need >60%.
- Cool aerosol: upper airway inflammation.
- Warm aerosol: dry gas injury prevention, mucociliary preservation.
- HME: avoid with secretions, high Ve, COPD, peds/neonates.
